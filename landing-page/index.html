1,784,659,392 bytes. In PowerShell:

Get-FileHash .\<downloaded file>.exe -Algorithm SHA256

Why is it 1.78 GB?

  • NVIDIA CUDA and PyTorch libraries — 3.8 GB of the 4.5 GB unpacked
  • A bundled Python runtime, so you install nothing
  • The speech model wrapper and a local web interface

The voice model itself is a separate 2.5 GB download on first launch.

Questions

Does it need internet?
Once, to fetch the voice model. Fully offline after that.
Is my audio uploaded anywhere?
No. Cloning and generation happen entirely on your machine.
Do I need a graphics card?
No, but without one expect many minutes per paragraph instead of seconds.
How fast is it?
On a modest 4 GB GPU, roughly four minutes of work per minute of speech.
How do I uninstall it?
Delete the folder. Nothing is written elsewhere.

If something goes wrong

“Windows protected your PC”
Expected — the app is unsigned. More info → Run anyway.
“Port 8000 is already in use”
Another program has it. Close that program, then reopen the app.
A “running on CPU” banner
No usable GPU was found. It still works, just slowly.
Output mumbles or skips words
Almost always the reference clip. Use 10–20 seconds, recorded dry and close to the mic.

Specifications

  • Output: MP3, 24 kHz mono
  • Reference clip: 2–60 seconds (10–20 s is best)
  • Script limit: 60,000 characters per job
  • Transcription of your sample is automatic
  • One job at a time — long scripts are split and stitched

One machine, one app

The download runs locally and listens only on your own computer — nothing is exposed to your network, and no firewall prompt appears.

Sharing it with other devices on your network is possible, but it is a separate server mode for people running from source, not this installer.
