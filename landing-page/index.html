Homegrown · v1.0.0

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • An NVIDIA graphics card — works without one, far slower
  • 8 GB free disk space
  • Internet on first launch only

Setting it up

  • Run the file, pick a folder to unpack into
  • Open that folder, run Homegrown.exe
  • Add a voice, paste a script, Generate

First launch fetches the 2.5 GB voice model, once.

For the best voice

  • 10–20 seconds of clean speech — longer is not better
  • Close to the mic, in a quiet, soft room — echo gets copied

Your sample is transcribed automatically.

Questions

Is my audio uploaded anywhere?
No. Cloning and generation happen entirely on your machine.
How fast is it?
On a modest 4 GB GPU, about four minutes of work per minute of speech.

Specifications

  • Output: MP3, 24 kHz mono
  • Reference clip: 2–60 s (10–20 s is best)
  • Script limit: 60,000 characters
  • Speaks 10 languages; long scripts are split and stitched

Uninstall by deleting the folder.
